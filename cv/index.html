About

Five years of taking ideas from a rough sketch to something running in production at global scale.

I've worked across media, telecoms, FMCG, healthcare, education and finance, usually as the person pulling together data science, engineering, marketing and ops teams and keeping them pointed in the same direction. Right now I'm a Senior Data Scientist and Project Lead at WPP Media, working with clients like Coca-Cola, Unilever, NBCUniversal and BT.

I enjoy solving business problems with a bit of creative thinking. Sometimes that means building a model, sometimes it's building the right tool, and sometimes it's just having the right conversation with the right person. I get as much satisfaction from the technical build as I do from reading a room and working out what a client genuinely needs.

Increasingly the work is less about writing the models myself and more about setting direction, mentoring the people who do, and translating all of it into decisions non-technical leaders can act on.
